Our story

From a tiny pizza oven to your favourite kitchen

DeliBites started with one wood-fired oven, a handful of family recipes and a simple belief: great food should be honest, generous and made from scratch.

Today our team of 40 cooks, bakers and riders serves thousands of guests every week — but every dish is still made the way we made the first one.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer vegetarian and vegan dishes?

Yes — around a third of our menu is vegetarian and we mark vegan dishes clearly.

What is your delivery area?

We deliver within 8 km of the restaurant. Enter your address at checkout to confirm.

Can I book for large groups?

Absolutely. For groups of 10+ please use the reservation form or call us.

Do you cater events?

We cater private and corporate events of up to 300 guests. Contact us for a quote.
