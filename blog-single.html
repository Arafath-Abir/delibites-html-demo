The Secret to Perfect Pasta Every Time

Nov 4, 2026Sofia Alvarez6 min read

Great pasta isn't about expensive ingredients. It's about three small habits that professional kitchens follow without thinking — and that anyone can bring home tonight.

1. Salt the water properly

Pasta absorbs water as it cooks, so the water is your only chance to season the noodle itself. Aim for about 10 grams of salt per litre.

“Pasta water should taste like a well-seasoned soup — never like the sea.”

2. Save the starch

Before draining, scoop out a mug of cooking water. That cloudy starch is what turns butter, cheese and oil into a glossy sauce that clings to every strand.

3. Finish in the pan

Pull the pasta a minute early and finish it in the sauce with a splash of pasta water. It will absorb flavour instead of just being coated by it.
